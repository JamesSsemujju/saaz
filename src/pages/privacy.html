Privacy

This website collects no personal data and uses no cookies or tracking. The free VIN decoder sends the VIN you type straight from your browser to the US NHTSA public decoder. Saaz does not store it.

Personal data

A full privacy notice is published before anyone can sign up. It will follow Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act. In short, Saaz plans to:

  • Register with the Personal Data Protection Office.
  • Ask for your phone number, not your ID. National ID is collected only from partner garage inspectors.
  • Ask your consent before storing data with a cloud provider outside Uganda.
  • Share your details with a lender only when you say yes.
  • Let you download or delete your account. Deleting it keeps the car's inspection history but removes the link to you.
