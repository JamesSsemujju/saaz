Privacy

If you create an account, Saaz stores your name, email and mobile number, and what you do on Saaz: the cars you list, their photos, offers, messages and inspection records. Saaz uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps your sign-in, any unsaved listing, and a random device number so a car's views are counted once a day. The free VIN decoder sends the VIN you type straight from your browser to the US NHTSA public decoder.

Personal data

A full privacy notice will follow Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act. In short, Saaz plans to:

  • Register with the Personal Data Protection Office.
  • Ask for your phone number, not your ID. National ID is collected only from partner garage inspectors.
  • Ask your consent before storing data with a cloud provider outside Uganda.
  • Share your details with a lender only when you say yes.
  • Let you download or delete your account. Deleting it keeps the car's inspection history but removes the link to you.
