Privacy

This website collects no personal data and uses no cookies or tracking.

When Saaz opens

A full privacy notice will be published before anyone can sign up. It will follow Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act. In short, Saaz plans to:

  • Register with the Personal Data Protection Office before launch.
  • Ask for your phone number, not your ID. National ID is collected only from partner garage inspectors.
  • Ask your consent before storing data with a cloud provider outside Uganda.
  • Share your details with a lender only when you say yes.
  • Let you download or delete your account. Deleting it keeps the car's inspection history but removes the link to you.

Draft. To be reviewed by a lawyer before launch.
