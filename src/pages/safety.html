Five checks before you pay for any car

General advice that protects you whether or not you use Saaz.

  1. 1
    Match the chassis numberThe number on the car must match the one on the logbook.
  2. 2
    Match the ownerThe name on the logbook should match the seller's ID.
  3. 3
    No deposit for a car you haven't seenHolding deposits are a common way to lose money.
  4. 4
    Meet at a garageNot at the roadside, and in daylight.
  5. 5
    Inspect before money changes handsA report in writing settles arguments before they start.

Saaz will never ask you to pay into a personal mobile money number.
