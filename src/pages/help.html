Questions

Answers come from the Saaz plan. Where something is not decided yet, we say so.

Buying
Who does the inspection?

Mechanics at partner garages that Saaz has checked: business papers, named inspectors, equipment, and a test inspection. Saaz does not employ mechanics.

What if the garage misses a fault?

You raise a dispute and the car gets a second inspection. If the dispute is upheld, the garage's rating drops, and its agreement with Saaz sets what it owes.

Can I check a car now?

You can decode a 17-character VIN for free on the Vehicle history page: make, model, year and engine, from the US NHTSA public decoder. It does not show history or condition, and it cannot read Japanese frame numbers or number plates yet.

What does the vehicle history show?

Every Saaz inspection of that car. It is not a police, URA or accident record.

Selling
Can I list my car on Saaz?

Yes, once it passes a Saaz inspection. The report is shown on the listing.

Who pays for the inspection?

The buyer, the seller, or both, chosen when the code is created.

Payments and data
How much will it cost?

The Saaz price is agreed with each partner garage. Payment is by {{payments}}.

What happens to my data?

Saaz will ask for your phone number, not your ID. Data will be stored with a cloud provider outside Uganda, and we will ask your consent for that when you sign up, as Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act requires. This website collects nothing.
