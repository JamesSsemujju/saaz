How Saaz works

One code, one partner garage, one report saved to the car's history. Here is the flow for each person involved.

Buyers

Know the car before you pay.

  1. 1
    Look the car upEnter its chassis number or plate to see earlier Saaz inspections.
  2. 2
    Get a code and bookChoose a partner garage. Agree with the seller who pays.
  3. 3
    Decide with the reportFindings and photos, shared on WhatsApp. No obligation to buy.
Sellers

Show buyers the car has nothing to hide.

  1. 1
    Get a code for your carPay yourself, or split the cost with the buyer.
  2. 2
    Pass the inspectionAt any partner garage. Standard photos are taken there.
  3. 3
    List it with the reportOnly cars that passed can be listed.
Partner garages

Careful work earns more bookings.

  1. 1
    Get checked and signPapers, named inspectors, equipment and a test inspection.
  2. 2
    Check the codeThe code must be valid, unused and match the car.
  3. 3
    Enter the reportOn a phone, even with weak signal. Your rating follows your work.
