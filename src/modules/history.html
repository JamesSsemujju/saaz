Decode a VIN for free.

See the factory make, model, year and engine for any 17-character VIN. This uses the public NHTSA decoder. It is not the car's history or condition.

The VIN you type is sent from your browser to the US NHTSA's public decoder. Saaz does not store it. Japanese frame numbers and number plates are not supported yet.

The record

What a vehicle history will show.

  • Every Saaz inspection of the car, newest first
  • The result, the findings and the photos from each one
  • Which partner garage inspected it, and when
  • Whether the chassis number matched the logbook
  • Any dispute and its outcome

The record only holds Saaz inspections. It is not a police, URA or accident record.
