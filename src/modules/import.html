The quote

Every cost in UGX, before you pay anything.

  • The car's price abroad
  • Shipping to Mombasa or Dar es Salaam, and transport to Kampala
  • URA taxes and levies, worked out from the car's age and value
  • Clearing agent fees
  • The Saaz fee, shown as its own line

Uganda's import rules can change. In 2026 a bill to lower the age limit from 15 to 13 years was withdrawn. Saaz keeps its tax table in one place and updates it when the rules change.

On arrival

Inspected before you collect it.

Every imported car gets a Saaz inspection at a partner garage when it reaches Kampala. The report starts the car's history in Uganda.
