Saaz Check

Get a free code for the car, take it to a Saaz partner garage, and get a written report with photos. Buyer and seller decide who pays, or split it.

For Buyers and sellers
How it works

Saaz Check in 3 steps.

  1. 1
    Get a free codeRegister with your phone number. One code per car, tied to its chassis number.
  2. 2
    Visit a partner garageThe garage checks the code and gives you the Saaz price.
  3. 3
    Get the reportChecks and photos on a link you can share on WhatsApp.

Our rules

What Saaz will promise every buyer and seller.

Independent inspectorsA garage never inspects a car it sells, repairs for the seller, or has a stake in.
Every finding in writingChecks and photos in one report, saved against the chassis number.
A second look if something was missedRaise a dispute and the car gets a second inspection. Upheld disputes lower the garage's rating.
No obligation to buyThe report is yours either way.
Never into a personal numberSaaz fees go to the Saaz merchant account only.
Ratings you can trustOnly buyers whose car was inspected can rate the garage.
