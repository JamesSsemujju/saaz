Partner garages

Saaz does not employ mechanics. Inspections happen at partner garages that Saaz has checked. Every garage and inspector has a public rating.

For Buyers and sellers

  3. 3
    Book with your Saaz codeThe garage gives you the Saaz price.
How garages are checked

Six steps before a garage goes live.

  • Business registration and trading licence
  • Named inspectors, each with ID and experience on file
  • A diagnostic scanner and a lift or pit
  • A test on cars with faults Saaz already knows about
  • Signed terms, including the rule against inspecting cars they have a stake in
  • First 10 reports checked by Saaz, then a mystery inspection every quarter
How ratings work

Ratings count more than stars.

  • Reviews only from buyers whose car the garage inspected
  • Upheld disputes: a later inspection found a fault this one missed
  • Complete reports: every check and photo done

A suspension is proposed by the system and signed off by a person. The garage can ask for a review.
