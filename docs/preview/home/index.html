How it works

Three steps from "I like this car" to knowing what you are buying.

  1. 1
    Get a free code for the carRegister with your phone number. One code per car, tied to its chassis number.
  2. 2
    Visit a partner garageThe garage checks the code and gives you the Saaz price.
  3. 3
    Get the reportChecks and photos on a link you can share on WhatsApp, saved to the car's history.

Questions
Who does the inspection?

Mechanics at partner garages that Saaz has checked. Saaz does not employ mechanics.

What if the garage misses a fault?

You raise a dispute and the car gets a second inspection. Upheld disputes lower the garage's rating.
