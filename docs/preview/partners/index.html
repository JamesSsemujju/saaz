Become a partner garage

Saaz sends buyers with a Saaz code to partner garages. Every partner is checked before going live, and rated after every job.

How it works

Become a partner garage in 3 steps.

  1. 1
    Get checkedBusiness papers, named inspectors, equipment, and a test inspection.
  2. 2
    Sign the partner termsThe checklist, the Saaz price, and what you owe for a missed fault.
  3. 3
    Take bookingsCheck each Saaz code, inspect, and enter the report on your phone.

Bookings from buyers who are ready to decide.
